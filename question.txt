### Impromptu Session

A book holds a house of gold

#### Introduction

In this impromptu session, we will explore how reading can enrich and enhance public speaking skills. 
Reading not only provides knowledge but also helps us learn new expressions and strengthen language organization skills, making our speeches more vivid and persuasive.

#### Objectives
- To stimulate members' interest in reading and recognize its importance in improving speaking skills.
- Through interactive questions, allow members to share their reading experiences and how they apply these experiences to their speeches.

#### Time Rules
- Minimum Required: 1 minutes
- Suggested: 1.5 minutes
- Must finished: 2 minutes

#### Tips
- Each participant will have 30s to think about the question.
- When speaking, try to combine personal experiences to make the content more authentic and persuasive.
- Make full use of body language and facial expressions during the speech.
- Usage of speak style is strongly encouraged.

### Questions

1. **Question 1:**
   - **Topic:** What is the last book you read, and how has it influenced your speaking style?
   - **Purpose:** To understand the books members have recently read and discuss their impact on speaking style.

2. **Question 2:**
   - **Topic:** What speaking techniques have you learned from reading that can be applied to public speaking?
   - **Purpose:** To guide members in sharing specific techniques learned from reading and discuss how to apply these techniques to speeches.

3. **Question 3:**
   - **Topic:** Which book do you think is most helpful for improving speaking skills, and why?
   - **Purpose:** To have members recommend books that have been helpful in improving speaking skills and explain why.

4. **Question 4:**
   - **Topic:** How do you use reading to prepare for a speech topic?
   - **Purpose:** To explore how members utilize reading resources to find and determine speech topics.

5. **Question 5:**
   - **Topic:** How do you usually record and organize useful information while reading?
   - **Purpose:** To understand how members effectively extract and record information from reading.

6. **Question 6:**
   - **Topic:** Have you ever quoted content from books in a speech? How did it go?
   - **Purpose:** To encourage members to share their experiences of quoting books in speeches and discuss the outcomes.

7. **Question 7:**
   - **Topic:** What potential benefits do you think reading fictional works have for public speaking?
   - **Purpose:** To explore the potential impact of fictional works on enhancing speakers' imagination, creativity, and emotional expression.

8. **Question 8:**
   - **Topic:** How do you balance reading with speech practice time? How important do you think this balance is for improving speaking skills?
   - **Purpose:** To discuss the role of time management in improving speaking skills and how to find a balance between reading and speech practice.
